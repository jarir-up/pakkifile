# ADR-0050: Personas drafted by agents, edited by the owner

- Status: Approved
- Approved by: Muhammad Jariruddin, 2026-11-10

## Context
ADR-0021 has the owner design personas and scenarios through a questionnaire. Drafts are faster to correct than a blank questionnaire is to answer.

## Decision
An agent drafts three persona fact tables: an Upwork developer paid through Payoneer, a designer paid directly by foreign clients, and a student with a sponsoring parent. Each one seeds known mismatches. The owner corrects or approves them in one pass before any evaluation set is built from them.

## Consequences
Refines ADR-0021: the owner still decides every persona, but starts from a draft.
